ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Built for the Wits Student Journey

University life is about more than attending lectures. Students balance assignments, examinations, campus events, societies, friendships and personal goals.

The Wits Campus Life Planner is a student project designed to make those responsibilities easier to organise. It helps students plan tasks, track progress and make better use of their time.
